⚠️ Why use ng-href instead of href="{{ }}"?

With href="{{ link }}", the browser briefly navigates to the literal "{{ link }}" URL before Angular processes the template — potentially causing a broken page load. ng-href defers the binding until Angular has resolved the expression.
